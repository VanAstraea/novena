UI fontthe sans for everything you read and click, beside Cormorant

Pairing theory: a serif like Cormorant pairs either with a humanist sans (same pen-drawn roots, so they agree) or a geometric sans (opposite construction, so they contrast cleanly). Avoid two fonts that are similar but not the same. All of these are free (SIL Open Font License) and can be hosted with the site.
